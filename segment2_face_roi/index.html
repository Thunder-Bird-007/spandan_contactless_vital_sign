Segment 2: Face Detection & ROI Extraction

This is the documentation site for Segment 2 of the Spandan project (contactless heart-rate & SpO2 estimation from ordinary video — EEE 312 DSP Lab, BUET). Segment 2 covers exactly one job: detect the face in each video frame, crop a forehead skin patch, and turn it into three raw signals — R(t), G(t), B(t) — plus the true sampling rate fs. Nothing here does filtering, CHROM/POS, FFT heart rate, or SpO2 — those are later segments.

The code

The actual implementation lives in the main project scaffold, not in this docs folder:

  • matlab/src/io/loadUBFCVideo.m — opens a subject's video, reads the true frame rate.
  • matlab/src/roi/extractROISignals.m — face detection, ROI cropping, per-frame R/G/B averaging.
  • matlab/scripts/run_segment2_roi_batch.m — batch driver the team runs over their assigned subjects.

What comes out of this segment

For every subject processed: data/processed/<subjectID>_rgb_traces.mat (fields R, G, B, fs, subjectID, numDroppedFrames) and results/figures/<subjectID>_roi_sanity.png — a sanity-check image with the detected face box and the final ROI box drawn on it. Always look at the PNG before trusting the .mat output — that's the whole point of it.
