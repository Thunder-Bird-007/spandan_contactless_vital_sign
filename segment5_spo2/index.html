Segment 5: SpO2 Ratio-of-Ratios & Empirical Calibration

This is the documentation site for Segment 5 of the Spandan project (contactless heart-rate & SpO2 estimation from ordinary video — EEE 312 DSP Lab, BUET). Segment 5 takes the raw and bandpass-filtered R(t), G(t), B(t) traces Segments 2–3 produced, computes an AC/DC ratio-of-ratios per subject, and empirically calibrates that ratio against real ground-truth SpO2 — first on DATASET_1's 5 subjects (a genuine but thin calibration attempt), then as a wider code-correctness sanity check against the Hoffman et al. finger-camera oximetry dataset. Nothing here does LOSO cross-validation, MAE/RMSE/ correlation metrics, or Bland-Altman plots — those are Segment 6.

The code

The actual implementation lives in the main project scaffold, not in this docs folder:

  • matlab/src/spo2/ratioOfRatios.m — AC/DC ratio-of-ratios computation.
  • matlab/src/spo2/calibrateSpO2.m — linear SpO2 = A − B×R fit/apply.
  • matlab/src/io/loadGroundTruth.m — DATASET_1/DATASET_2 ground-truth parsing (implemented as part of this segment, since the DATASET_1 calibration needs timestamp-based SpO2 alignment).
  • matlab/scripts/run_segment5_dataset1_calibration_batch.m — DATASET_1 leave-one-out calibration driver.
  • matlab/scripts/run_segment5_hoffman_sanity_check.m — Hoffman dataset code-correctness sanity check.

What comes out of this segment

results/metrics/segment5_dataset1_calibration.csv — 5 leave-one-out rows (subjectID, R_value, SpO2_true, SpO2_predicted, abs_error), mean absolute error 1.97 percentage points across a narrow ~96–99% true-SpO2 range. results/metrics/ segment5_hoffman_sanity_check.csv — 351 held-out test-window rows from the Hoffman dataset, test MAE 8.56 percentage points across a genuinely wide ~65–100% range. Two sanity PNGs in results/figures/: segment5_dataset1_calibration_ scatter.png (predicted vs. actual, labeled by subject, y=x reference line) and segment5_hoffman_sanity_scatter.png (a two-panel figure — pooled R vs. SpO2 with the fitted line, and the same points colored per subject, since the pooled trend alone understates the real per-subject relationship). Always look at these before trusting the numbers — see the Line-by-Line document for exactly what to look for and why the per-subject panel exists.
